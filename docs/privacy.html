MyTVPlayer privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

MyTVPlayer is a TV app for watching live channels, catch-up, movies and series from IPTV services you set up yourself. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it never sends your information to the developer. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your playlists: the server address, user name and password of each IPTV service you add, or the address of an M3U playlist.
  • Your choices: the categories and channels you show or hide, favourites, TV guide sources and the guide you picked for each channel, and app settings.
  • Viewing history: recently watched channels and how far you got into movies and episodes, so you can carry on where you left off.
  • Downloaded lists: the channel, movie and series lists and TV guide data the app downloads from the services you set up.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Who the app connects to

To work, the app connects directly from your device to:

  • The IPTV services you add. The app signs in to them with the details you entered, and downloads their channel, movie and series lists, TV guide, catch-up listings and video streams.
  • TV guide (XMLTV) addresses you add, and, if you choose them, free country guides from iptv-epg.org.
  • iptv-org (iptv-org.github.io), only if you choose the free channels.
  • Image servers named in those lists, to show channel logos and posters, and the stream servers the lists point to.

Like any internet connection, these services receive your device's IP address and the app's technical request details (such as its name and version). What they do with them is covered by their own privacy policies. The app does not send your information to anyone else.

Backups you make

Settings › Backup & restore can save your set-up, including playlist logins, to a file in a place you choose on your device. The file is only created when you ask for it, and only you decide where it goes. Keep it private, as it contains your logins.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server of its own, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. No information is sold, shared or used for tracking.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download lists, guides and video, and to notice when the connection is down.

Deleting your information

Deleting a playlist (Settings › Playlists › Edit › Delete) removes its logins, downloaded lists, favourites and viewing history. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Backup files you made stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
