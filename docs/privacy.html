MyPlexPlayer privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

MyPlexPlayer is a TV app for watching films, TV shows and Live TV from your own Plex Media Server. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: the app has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it never sends your information to the developer. It talks only to Plex and to your own servers.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your Plex sign-in: the token Plex gives the app when you enter its code at plex.tv/link, and each of your servers' addresses and access tokens.
  • Your choices: app settings, the libraries and channels you show or hide, and your favourite films and series.
  • A copy of your libraries: the film, series, channel and TV guide lists the app reads from your servers, and how far you got in each title, so screens open quickly.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app, and it is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Who the app connects to

To work, the app connects directly from your device to:

  • plex.tv (Plex, Inc.): to sign you in with a code, to find your servers, and to read and save your favourite Live TV channels, which Plex keeps with your Plex account.
  • Your Plex Media Servers: to read your libraries, the TV guide and your recordings, to play video, and to save what you watched and how far you got, as every Plex app does. When you choose them, the app also asks your server to record a programme or a series, or to delete a title.
  • Image servers that your Plex server names, to show posters, pictures and channel logos.

Like any internet connection, these services receive your device's IP address and the app's technical request details (such as its name and version). What Plex does with them is covered by Plex's privacy policy. The app does not send your information to anyone else.

Backups and problem reports

Settings › Backup & restore can save your settings to a file in a place you choose on your device. The file is only made when you ask for it, and only you decide where it goes.

If the app stops unexpectedly, it can offer to open a GitHub issue with details of the problem. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and your Plex sign-in is left out.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server of its own, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. No information is sold, shared or used for tracking.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to talk to Plex and your servers, and to notice when the connection is down, or whether a VPN is connected when you turn on that setting.
  • Storage (Android 9 only): to save and open backup files, only when you ask.

Deleting your information

Removing a server (Settings › Playlists) removes its copy of the libraries from the device. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything it stored, the Plex sign-in included. Backup files you made stay where you saved them until you delete them. What you watched is kept on your Plex server, which you manage in Plex.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
